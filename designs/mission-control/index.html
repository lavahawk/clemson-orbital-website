Where we came from

A CubeSat team that outgrew its name.

Clemson CubeSat showed that a small group of students could build flight hardware, test it honestly, and keep it running after launch.

Clemson Orbital keeps that team and its habits. The new name covers the bigger missions, the research partnerships, and the outreach we're adding on top.

How a mission runs

Six phases, and each one ends in a review.

  1. 01Define. Set the goal, the requirements, and what success looks like.
  2. 02Design. Size the spacecraft and check every interface.
  3. 03Build. Fabricate, wire, and program.
  4. 04Test. Vibration, thermal vacuum, and functional checks.
  5. 05Launch. Integrate with a launch provider.
  6. 06Operate. Command it and get the data home.
