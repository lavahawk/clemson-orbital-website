Missions

Every mission starts on a whiteboard and ends in orbit.

A mission is the whole life of a spacecraft: the question it answers, the hardware that answers it, and the operations that keep it useful after launch.

03 / 06 34.68° N, 82.84° W
Lifecycle

Six phases, and each one ends in a review.

We don't start a phase until the last one has a written record. Members and outside advisors can sit in on any review.

01 / 06

Define

Set the goal, the requirements, and what success looks like in plain terms.

02 / 06

Design

Size the spacecraft, pick the subsystems, and check every interface before anyone cuts metal.

03 / 06

Build

Fabricate, wire, and program, keeping drawings and the parts list current.

04 / 06

Test

Vibration, thermal vacuum, and functional checks. Better to find failures on the bench.

05 / 06

Launch

Prepare for integration with a launch provider and support the spacecraft through deployment.

06 / 06

Operate

Command it, pull down the data, and get results to whoever asked for them.
