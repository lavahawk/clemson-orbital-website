04 / 06Research

A lab that has to survive a rocket launch.

Orbit gives researchers conditions that are hard to fake on the ground. We help get experiments there, run them safely, and bring the data home.

01
How it works

You bring the question. We make it flyable.

Researchers tell us what they need to measure. We handle the engineering around it: mechanical fit, power budget, data handling, and the safety reviews launch providers require.

Students on a payload team see an experiment through every stage, and faculty get a flight platform they couldn't justify building alone.

02
Areas

Where we focus

01

Space environment

Radiation, thermal swings, and drag, measured in place to check the models engineers rely on.

02

Small satellite systems

Attitude control, power, and software that has to run on very little hardware.

03

Sensing and imaging

Payloads that look at Earth or near space, with an eye on data regional partners can use.

04

Communications

Link budgets and ground stations for a spacecraft that's only overhead for a few minutes at a time.
