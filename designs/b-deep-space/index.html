Student aerospace at Clemson University

Clemson's student space program, built to fly.

Clemson Orbital started as Clemson CubeSat. We still build satellites, and we've taken on more: our own missions to orbit, a way for researchers to get experiments up there, and a louder voice for aerospace in South Carolina and the Southeast.

Advocacy

Where we came from

A CubeSat team that outgrew its name.

Clemson CubeSat showed that a small group of students could build flight hardware, test it honestly, and keep it running after launch.

Clemson Orbital keeps that team and its habits. The new name covers the bigger missions, the research partnerships, and the outreach we're adding on top.
