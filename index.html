Clemson Orbital is a student-led initiative bringing aerospace to Clemson students: research opportunities, help starting new projects, and real-world experience for every major.

see what we do

one goal, several programs.

the goal

Space at Clemson: a lasting program where students design spacecraft, run real research, and stay in South Carolina to do it.

the programs

Clemson CubeSat is where members design and build research satellites. X-PLORE brings that work to middle and high schools. Both run under Clemson Orbital.

read our story

what we do

missionswhat we're building

Our first spacecraft are CubeSats, designed by student teams on campus. Nothing of ours has flown yet, so the work right now is requirements, design and testing, run through six phases that each end in a written review.

how a mission works
researchwhat we study

Every satellite starts with a research question. Students choose one worth sending to orbit, then do the engineering around it: power, mass, data and the reviews a launch provider expects. Any major can join a research team.

research with us
advocacywhat we argue for

Students here shouldn't have to leave the Southeast to work in space. We table at events and visit schools, and we want local employers in our reviews and our results in front of the people deciding on research funding and workforce programs in South Carolina. Through X-PLORE, middle and high school students spend a semester working as a spacecraft engineering team.

about X-PLORE

six reviews to orbit

We don't start a phase until the last one has a written record. Members and outside advisors can sit in on any review.

write to the whole team

Joining, a sponsorship, X-PLORE or a school visit: it all lands at cubesat@clemson.edu, and we usually reply within a week during the semester.
